Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

At OmniDownloader, accessible from http://localhost:3000, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by OmniDownloader and how we use it. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

Zero Data Retention Policy

OmniDownloader is a local-first web utility wrapper. We do not operate databases, user accounts, or tracking systems. We do not store, analyze, or compile logs of the URLs you paste, the files you download, or the metadata generated. When you submit a link for analysis, all operations are processed in memory or written temporarily to a secure project directory on the local host machine, and deleted immediately once the download is transmitted back to your browser.

Information We Collect

While we do not compile databases of user logs, standard network and browser operations may trigger temporary data storage:

  • Local Storage (Browser-Side Only): Our application uses your web browser's localStorage database to store your "Recent Downloads" dashboard. This list is saved locally on your own machine. We have zero access to this history, and you can clear it completely at any time by clicking the "Clear History" button.
  • Log Files: OmniDownloader follows a standard procedure of using log files on the local server console. These logs include Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click paths. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are strictly used to troubleshoot server runtimes locally.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Vendor Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense and other third-party vendors to serve advertisements on our website to support our maintenance and hosting. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.

Google's DART Cookie

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to OmniDownloader and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ad Settings: Users can manage how Google serves personalized ads by visiting the Google Ads Settings Page.
  • AboutAds Opt-out Portal: Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, OmniDownloader uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Our Advertising Partners

Some of the advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partners are listed below. Each of our advertising partners has their own Privacy Policy for their policies on user data. For easier access, we hyperlinked to their Privacy Policies below:

Third-Party Privacy Policies

OmniDownloader's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. To know more detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers, it can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

GDPR and CCPA Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – You have the right to request copies of your personal data. (Since we store no personal data, any such request will return empty).
  • The right to rectification – You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure – You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity.

OmniDownloader does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
