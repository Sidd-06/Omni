Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

This is the Cookie Policy for OmniDownloader, accessible from http://localhost:3000. Like most professional platforms, this site uses cookies and browser local storage to provide and improve your experience. This document outlines what information they gather, how we use it, and why we sometimes need to store these files.

What Are Cookies?

As is common practice with almost all professional websites, this site uses cookies, which are tiny files that are downloaded to your computer, to improve your experience. This page describes what information they gather, how we use it and why we sometimes need to store these cookies. We will also share how you can prevent these cookies from being stored however this may downgrade or 'break' certain elements of the sites functionality.

How We Use Cookies & Local Storage

We use cookies for a variety of reasons detailed below. Unfortunately, in most cases, there are no industry standard options for disabling cookies without completely disabling the functionality and features they add to this site. It is recommended that you leave on all cookies if you are not sure whether you need them or not in case they are used to provide a service that you use.

1. First-Party Cookies & Browser Storage

  • LocalStorage Database (Recent Downloads): Rather than standard cookies, our application uses HTML5 localStorage to store your "Recent Downloads" list locally on your computer. This list allows the application to show previously downloaded items without needing to store your data on external servers. You can wipe this local cache instantly by clicking the "Clear History" button in the history card.
  • Session Settings: We may use session cookies to remember layout choices (like active tabs) as you navigate the interface. These are destroyed the moment you close your browser tab.

2. Third-Party Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

In addition to our first-party tools, third-party services may set cookies in your browser. We use Google AdSense to serve contextual ads to support maintenance costs:

  • Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • These cookies track web browsing behaviors to serve ads that are relevant to your interests (interest-based advertising).

You can opt out of personalized third-party advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out portal at www.aboutads.info.

Disabling Cookies

You can prevent the setting of cookies by adjusting the settings on your browser (see your browser Help for how to do this). Be aware that disabling cookies will affect the functionality of this and many other websites that you visit. Disabling cookies will usually result in also disabling certain functionality and features of this site. Therefore it is recommended that you do not disable cookies.

More Information

  • Form: Navigate to our Contact Us page.
